Questions

Do you do the excavation as well as the concrete?

Yes. Site cuts, trenching, removing old concrete, carting spoil and preparing the sub-base are all part of what we do. That is the point: one crew from the dig to the wash-off.

Where do you work?

Across the Sunshine Coast and surrounding areas, and we also take on work on the Central Coast in NSW. Send your suburb with the quote form and we will confirm.

What finishes can I choose from?

Plain, coloured, exposed aggregate, honed and spray-on finishes, plus custom decorative work. We will talk through what suits your home and how you use the space before we pour.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Elite holds QBCC licence 15591807 and is fully insured, with work health and safety procedures on every site.

Do you work for builders?

We do. Elite subcontracts on residential and light-commercial projects, including slabs, formwork, steel fixing and concrete cutting. Send your plans through and we will come back with pricing.
